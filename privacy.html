Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026 • Effective Date: October 2, 2026
🔒 The Zero-Telemetry Guarantee ZapTextCleaner operates entirely on a 100% client-side architecture. When you paste text, documents, code snippets, or book excerpts into any tool on this site, your data is processed entirely inside your local device's browser memory (RAM). Your text is never transmitted to, inspected by, or stored on any external server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Text & Documents)

Unlike conventional web applications that process string manipulation and format conversion on cloud-hosted backends, ZapTextCleaner does not operate backend application servers for text processing:

  • No Clipboard Logging: Any text pasted into the input editors never leaves your browser sandbox.
  • No Server-Side File Storage: We do not operate databases, S3 storage buckets, or log files that retain user-submitted content.
  • No Model Training: Your input data is never used to train machine learning models, statistical datasets, or artificial intelligence systems.
  • Instant Eviction: Refreshing or closing your browser tab immediately flushes all submitted text from your system's temporary memory (RAM).

2. Technical Architecture & Local Execution

All core functionalities across our tool suites—including whitespace normalization, HTML entity decoding, line wrap stripping, and regex formatting—are implemented using native JavaScript APIs (such as DOMParser, String.prototype.replace(), and the Clipboard API). Execution occurs locally in your web browser engine (V8, SpiderMonkey, or WebKit) without firing asynchronous HTTP POST payloads.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

ZapTextCleaner does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal profile registrations. We do not set first-party tracking cookies. Third-party advertising partners and analytics services may set standard operational cookies to measure ad impressions and site engagement according to their independent privacy standards.

5. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Because user text data is processed exclusively on the client side, ZapTextCleaner never acts as a Data Processor or Data Controller for the contents of your copied documents.
  • We hold no personally identifiable records, customer contact databases, or archived document streams.
  • Users hold sole custody and control of their text data throughout the entire editing session.
